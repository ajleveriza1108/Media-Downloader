## Installed updates

Installed copies select the release-repository installer EXE and require its stable-manifest SHA-256 before launch.

## Portable updates

Portable copies contain MediaDock.portable.

They select the release-repository portable ZIP, verify its SHA-256, close the running app, stage and smoke-test the new portable payload, back up the current portable folder, apply the new payload to the same folder, and relaunch.

Failure during replacement restores the portable backup.
