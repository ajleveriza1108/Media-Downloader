# R1.6.19 - Installer-Only Release Updater

## Distribution

Customer artifact:

MediaDock-Setup-R1.6.19-Trial.exe

The installer is per-user and installs under Local AppData without Administrator elevation.

## Trial and licensing

Unlicensed allowance:

- Video: 5 successful outputs;
- MP3: 5 successful outputs.

The License access entry remains available before trial exhaustion.

A customer who activates a valid license keeps the same installation; no separate full-version executable is required.

Updates do not reset trial/license state.

## Installer updater

The app reads only:

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajleveriza1108/MediaDock-Release/main/latest-stable.json

Contract version: schema 3 / installer-only.

The updater rejects development metadata, inactive stable pointers, same/older versions, non-MediaDock release URLs, non-EXE artifacts, malformed SHA-256 values, and unpublished installers.
