## Playlist queue workflow

When a supported playlist is pasted, MediaDock automatically analyzes it and loads eligible entries directly into Download Queue.

In trial mode, playlist admission is capped by the remaining allowance for the selected output format. The user is notified when items are skipped because of the trial limit, duplication, or unavailable/invalid entries.
