- Replaced the update notification with a cleaner premium card, subtle motion, Update Now/Later actions, and customer-friendly copy.
- Removed technical update-channel language from the customer-facing Settings update area.
- Preserves the accepted R1.6.35 Settings behavior, R1.6.33 premium Convert workspace, queue behavior, licensing, concurrency, original-audio preference, and output-folder behavior.
